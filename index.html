About this map

The Amazon Basin is a free, non-commercial tool for studying the Amazon river system. It maps the basin's roughly 745,000 river reaches from the HydroRIVERS network and reveals satellite imagery only along their corridors, so the drainage pattern itself becomes the map. Every named river carries measurements from the RiverATLAS database, and towns of 2,000 people or more can be examined alongside them. It is intended for education and research; discharge, population and flooding figures are modelled long-term averages rather than gauge readings, and river names follow national usage where sources disagree.
